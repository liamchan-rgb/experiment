# Right-facing battle idle short sword · single-pose candidate v1

This directory contains an original pixel-geometric weapon study registered to the unchanged 256×256 `battle-side-right / idle / 00` pose. The SVG is the editable master; `weapon.png` is its transparent full-canvas export. The blade extends about 44 px toward the upper-right at approximately −22°, with a narrow silver-white steel silhouette, a brown grip, and warm-gold guard and pommel.

The grip center uses the candidate landmark `(145.566, 163.681)` recorded in `Art/Templates/P1-Neutral/battle-side/right-idle-r2/registration.json`. The pommel sits toward the pinky-side lower-left; the guard sits outside the thumb/index side upper-right. The guard wings share one foreshortened diagonal. The weapon is composed behind the unchanged r2 body so the near hand covers the middle of the grip; see [local hand review](review-local.png) and [full registered assembly](assembled-r2.png).

This is one unapproved art candidate. The approved r2 scope covers an empty-hand idle pose and hand direction only. This preview checks registration and overlap; it cannot prove a convincing grip, balance, animation, another view, reusable weapon compatibility, or runtime behavior. No body artwork or app code was changed.

Socket and orientation metadata are in [socket.json](socket.json). The image was drawn from original SVG geometry and rasterized on the shared canvas; it was not extracted from the approved scene composite.
