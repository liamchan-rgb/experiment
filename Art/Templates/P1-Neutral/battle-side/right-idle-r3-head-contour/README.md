# P1 right-side idle · r3 rear-head contour candidate

The user requested a less rounded back of the head in the battle appearance. This is a common neutral-head contour revision derived from the retained r2 pose, on the same 256×256 canvas. It is not a body selected for a particular appearance ID.

`head-mask.svg` trims the rear skull/nape and `head-edge.svg` restores its stepped outline. `build.sh` reproduces `00.png` from the unchanged r2 original. The 97 changed pixels are restricted to `[101,105,116,127)`; everything at and below y=128, the face/ear region at x≥117, pose landmarks and foot registration are unchanged. See `validation.json`.

The r2 pose approval remains limited to r2. This r3 head contour is unapproved, used only by the current isolated art candidate, and has not been imported into the app.
